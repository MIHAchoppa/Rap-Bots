# $SHELL Basics

## Introduction to $SHELL

$SHELL is the native token powering the MyShell ecosystem, granting access to premium features, enabling creator support, and facilitating payments.

Our thriving ecosystem is powered by three key AI players: creators, consumers, and investors. These roles are interdependent, supporting each other and collectively forming our vast ecosystem. $SHELL will be strategically distributed as the MyShell ecosystem grows and evolves.

The total supply of $SHELL is capped at **1,000,000,000 tokens**, which will be gradually released over time to cultivate a deflationary economy and maintain token scarcity.

### Distribution Categories Explained

**Ecosystem/Treasury**: Tokens are allocated to the treasury for purposes such as onboarding strategic partners, developing the on-chain ecosystem, and facilitating special governance proposals.

**Community Incentive**: Tokens are allocated as incentives for the community. Distributed seasonally, these tokens reward community users for completing seasonal tasks, creating AI apps, providing liquidity, and other activities that engage consumers, creators, and patrons.
